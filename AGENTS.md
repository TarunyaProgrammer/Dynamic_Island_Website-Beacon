| Dimension | What Beacon Sounds Like | What Beacon NEVER Sounds Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Apple-grade precision, confident, understated, punchy | Bubbly corporate optimism, robotic AI fluff, generic hype |
| **Philosophy** | "Pay once, own forever. Your data stays on your Mac." | "Subscribe to our cloud-native enterprise platform." |
| **Features** | "6 Behavioral Paradigms engineered for real humans." | "A comprehensive task management solution." |
| **Technical Credibility**| "0.1% idle CPU. SQLite WAL. Sub-second Gemini cascade." | "Powered by advanced patented AI algorithms." |

### Tone Guardrails (Anti-AI Slop Rules)
1. **Never use generic buzzwords**: Banned words: *streamline, leverage, synergy, game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, unleash, superpower*.
2. **Lead with hardware & tactile feeling**: Use words like *chassis, obsidian, notch, tactile, glanceable, zero-distance, air-gapped*.
3. **Respect developer intelligence**: Always pair claims with technical proof (e.g. not just "it's fast" -> "rendered with 0ms popover delay and 45MB RAM").
